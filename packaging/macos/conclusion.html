LogAnalyser is ready

  1. Open LogAnalyser from your Applications folder or Launchpad.
  2. Drop a folder of log files onto its window, or click Choose folder.
  3. Click Analyse. You get the most likely cause, the lines that prove it, and what to try.

An AI second opinion is optional. Turn it on in Settings, with an online AI or with Ollama, which runs on this Mac.

To remove LogAnalyser later, drag it from Applications to the Trash.
