Welcome to LogAnalyser

LogAnalyser reads log files and tells you the most likely reason something failed, with the lines that prove it.

This installer will:

  • check that this Mac can run LogAnalyser: macOS 11 or later, a Mac with Apple silicon, and about 300 MB of free space
  • put LogAnalyser in your Applications folder

Nothing else needs to be installed. Your Mac will ask for your password, as it does for any app installed into Applications.
